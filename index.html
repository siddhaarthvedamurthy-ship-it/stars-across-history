About Precession

Earth's axis wobbles like a spinning top, completing one full cycle every ~25,772 years — the Platonic Year. This shifts which star sits nearest the celestial pole. Thuban (α Draconis) was the pole star around 2750 BCE; Polaris holds the title today; Vega will take the crown near 14,000 CE.
