About Precession

Earth's axis wobbles like a spinning top, completing one full cycle every ~25,772 years — the Platonic Year — while the 23.4° axial tilt itself breathes between 22.1° and 24.5° over a 41,000-year cycle. Together they decide which star sits nearest each celestial pole. Thuban ruled the north around 2750 BCE; Polaris holds it today; Errai takes over near 4000 CE. Over this 100,000-year atlas the pole sweeps the whole map.
